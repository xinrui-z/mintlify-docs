## Notes

- Monthly limits differ by model. Models with a lower cap draw down the shared quota faster; models with a higher cap draw it down more slowly.
- Usage caps are not separate allowances: all models share one quota, and each cap only shows what that model alone can use. The caps don't add up across models.
- Models with an input range are priced in tiers: once a single request's total input (input + cache reads + cache writes) exceeds the threshold, the whole request is billed at the higher tier.
- API usage is priced at the rates in the table. The model’s quota multiplier determines how much of the shared subscription quota it uses.
- When you pay from your account balance instead of the subscription quota, requests are charged at the full prices listed above.

Prices and caps may change as the lineup evolves. The [Harogo console](https://console.harogo.ai/plan) and this page are the source of truth.

## Usage and Limits {#usage-limits}

Harogo enforces the following usage limits:

- Daily limit: \$12 (resets every day at **16:00 UTC**)
- Weekly limit: \$30 (resets every 7 days from when you first use the subscription)
- Monthly limit: \$60 (resets every 30 days from when you first use the subscription)

All three limits apply at the same time. Each model has its own usage cap: models capped below \$60 draw down the shared quota faster, and models capped above \$60 draw it down more slowly. See [Pricing](#token-prices) for each model's cap.

Limits are defined in dollar amounts rather than a fixed request count. How many requests you actually get depends on the model you use — lower-priced models generally support more requests, while higher-priced models support fewer.

You can check your current usage and remaining quota in the [Harogo console](https://console.harogo.ai/plan).

### Subscription and balance API keys {#billing-keys}

Create separate keys on the [API Keys page](https://console.harogo.ai/keys): a subscription key to use your plan quota, and a balance key to pay from your account balance. The two are tracked separately. If your subscription quota runs out, Harogo does not automatically charge your balance.
