## Notes

- **GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna** — two rates apply depending on the context length of each request.
- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, DeepSeek V4 Pro** — peak and off-peak hours are billed at different rates. The prices shown are peak rates.
- **DeepSeek V4 Pro** has a \$15 monthly cap. Every other model is capped at \$60.

Prices and caps may change as the lineup evolves. The [Harogo console](https://console.harogo.ai/plan) and this page are the source of truth.
